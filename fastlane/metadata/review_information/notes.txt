No sign-in is needed. Listen live: the Listen tab's Listen live button. Notifications are off until turned on in Settings (show reminders, radio alarm, Notifications from WXPN). The radio alarm schedules a notification; tapping it starts the station (a notification can't start audio on its own). Donate opens xpn.org/donate in the browser view, as App Review 3.2.2 allows for nonprofits. Playlist sync signs in with Spotify or Apple Music only if the listener chooses to.
