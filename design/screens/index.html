Lock screen

The phone draws these itself, from what the app hands it: the song, artist and artwork while the station plays, the radio alarm as a notification, and the live video and member drive notifications a listener turns on in Settings. A live station has no skip, so the system shows play and pause only.

  • iPhone, playing

    Now Playing on the lock screen, with AirPlay.

  • iPhone, alarm

    Tapping it opens the app and starts the station.

  • iPhone, notifications

    Live video and member drives, for listeners who turned them on.

  • Android, playing

    The media card takes the song’s artwork.

  • Android, alarm

    In the station’s orange, from the app’s notification icon.

  • Android, notifications

    Tapping opens the video, or the donate page.
